\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{General Evaluation}\par

\textbf{1. Work Environment / Team Culture}\\
The working environment is very friendly and open. Members of FCAJ are
always ready to support me when I encounter difficulties, even outside
working hours. The workspace is neat and comfortable. Despite being an
intern, I feel respected and treated as an official member of the team.

\textbf{2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin}\\
The mentor provides very detailed guidance, explains clearly when I
don't understand, and always encourages me to ask questions. The team
admin supports with procedures, documents, and creates conditions for me
to work smoothly.

\textbf{3. Alignment between Work and Major}\\
I am studying Computer Science at school and feel it is very suitable
when approaching AWS services. The work not only gives me excitement for
new technical issues but also allows me to apply the knowledge I learned
at school.

\textbf{4. Opportunities for Learning \& Skill Development}\\
During the internship, I was trained in self-learning skills, research
skills, and working with AWS services. Besides, I also learned how to
write reports and present work results, which are very essential skills
for students.

\textbf{5. Culture \& Team Spirit}\\
It feels great to work in an open and dynamic environment. Everyone is
willing to share and help each other when facing difficulties, creating
a comfortable and effective working atmosphere. This helps me integrate
quickly and feel attached to the team.

\textbf{6. Policies / Benefits for Interns}\\
I feel that the policies for interns are very reasonable and support
learning and training. In particular, I really like the weekly Workshops
organized to help program members learn from highly skilled seniors and
experts in the industry.
