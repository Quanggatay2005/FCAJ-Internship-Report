\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Key Highlights}\par

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Introduction to the current IT job market and future career
trends}\par

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  The AI trend is changing how we work, requiring new skills and new
  approaches.
\item
  The shift from on-premise to cloud-native and hybrid cloud.
\item
  The transition from role-based jobs to goal-based / outcome-based
  jobs, with distinct differences across various industry groups
  (Banking, Fintech, Retail\ldots).
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Differences between school projects and enterprise projects -
``Same skills, different
game''}\par

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  For schools, the goal is to learn how to deploy systems or solve
  specific problems.
\item
  For enterprises, the goal is to satisfy customers while optimizing
  costs and deployment time.
\item
  There are no clear documents or guidelines about requirements; you
  must proactively research and clarify the problems to be solved.
\item
  The difference between tutorial and production:

  \begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
    Tutorial: You are the one creating the rules
  \item
    Production: You must follow the rules set by others
  \end{itemize}
\item
  The speaker shared experiences from working at various companies,
  ranging from enterprises to startups.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Communication skills in the workplace and working with
clients}\par

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Requires active interaction among you, team members, and clients
\item
  Communication skill is one of the most important skills in the
  corporate environment
\item
  Communication must be clear, especially regarding work; problems and
  solutions must be clearly presented
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Essential skills and tools in the AI
era}\par

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  You need to differentiate yourself from the crowd
\item
  You must have ``Integrity'' - meaning you should do your best on your
  projects.
\item
  Introduction to 5 benefits of a job:

  \begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
    Compensation and benefits
  \item
    Skill development
  \item
    Networking
  \item
    Acquired knowledge
  \item
    Growth potential \textgreater{} Don't just focus on finding a
    high-paying job; pay attention to the other 4 factors to build a
    good and suitable career for yourself in the future.
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{What I Learned}\par

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Current job market
requirements}\par

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Core foundations}: still crucial in today's AI era
\item
  \textbf{The job market changes every day}: you must adapt yourself to
  meet the requirements
\item
  \textbf{Side-projects need to evolve}: thanks to AI, projects must
  meet higher quality standards and be capable of running in a
  production environment.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Differences between school and
enterprise}\par

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Requires higher-level thinking compared to school projects: not just
  solving a problem, but also optimizing costs, time, and satisfying
  customers.
\item
  Need to build a very detailed plan: requires a clear roadmap, deep
  understanding of the domain, thorough research on application
  features, and strict compliance with enterprise rules.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Soft skills}\par

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Communication skills remain paramount in the corporate environment
\item
  Sometimes you shouldn't be too rigid at work; flexibility is needed to
  balance mutual benefits.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\textbf{Essential skills and tools in the AI
era}\par

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  AI is just a tool, it cannot replace human thinking.
\item
  You must have core skills to be able to identify AI's mistakes.
\end{itemize}
