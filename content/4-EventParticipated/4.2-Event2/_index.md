# Event "AWS: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Application featuring Cloud Kinetics & Renova Cloud"

### Event Objectives

- Introduce the current IT job market and future employment trends
- Understand the differences in requirements and working environments between school and enterprise
- Provide advice on soft skills, especially effective communication skills in the workplace
- Essential skills in the AI era

### List of Speakers

- **Nguyen Gia Hung** - Head of Solution Architect in Vietnam & Cambodia, AWS
- **Khang Nguyen** - Solutions Architect, Cloud Kinetics
- **Nhu Tran** - Account Manager, AWS
- **Vinh Banh** - Senior Data Engineer, Renova Cloud

### Key Highlights

#### Introduction to the current IT job market and future career trends
- The AI trend is changing how we work, requiring new skills and new approaches.
- The shift from on-premise to cloud-native and hybrid cloud.
- The transition from role-based jobs to goal-based / outcome-based jobs, with distinct differences across various industry groups (Banking, Fintech, Retail...).

#### Differences between school projects and enterprise projects - "Same skills, different game"
- For schools, the goal is to learn how to deploy systems or solve specific problems.
- For enterprises, the goal is to satisfy customers while optimizing costs and deployment time.
- There are no clear documents or guidelines about requirements; you must proactively research and clarify the problems to be solved.
- The difference between tutorial and production: 
    + Tutorial: You are the one creating the rules
    + Production: You must follow the rules set by others 
- The speaker shared experiences from working at various companies, ranging from enterprises to startups. 

#### Communication skills in the workplace and working with clients
- Requires active interaction among you, team members, and clients
- Communication skill is one of the most important skills in the corporate environment 
- Communication must be clear, especially regarding work; problems and solutions must be clearly presented

#### Essential skills and tools in the AI era
- You need to differentiate yourself from the crowd
- You must have "Integrity" - meaning you should do your best on your projects. 
- Introduction to 5 benefits of a job: 
    + Compensation and benefits
    + Skill development
    + Networking
    + Acquired knowledge
    + Growth potential
> Don't just focus on finding a high-paying job; pay attention to the other 4 factors to build a good and suitable career for yourself in the future.



### What I Learned

#### Current job market requirements
- **Core foundations**: still crucial in today's AI era
- **The job market changes every day**: you must adapt yourself to meet the requirements
- **Side-projects need to evolve**: thanks to AI, projects must meet higher quality standards and be capable of running in a production environment. 

#### Differences between school and enterprise
- Requires higher-level thinking compared to school projects: not just solving a problem, but also optimizing costs, time, and satisfying customers.
- Need to build a very detailed plan: requires a clear roadmap, deep understanding of the domain, thorough research on application features, and strict compliance with enterprise rules.

#### Soft skills
- Communication skills remain paramount in the corporate environment
- Sometimes you shouldn't be too rigid at work; flexibility is needed to balance mutual benefits. 

#### Essential skills and tools in the AI era
- AI is just a tool, it cannot replace human thinking. 
- You must have core skills to be able to identify AI's mistakes.
