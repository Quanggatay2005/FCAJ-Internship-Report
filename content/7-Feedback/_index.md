### General Evaluation

**1. Work Environment / Team Culture**  
The working environment is very friendly and open. Members of FCAJ are always ready to support me when I encounter difficulties, even outside working hours. The workspace is neat and comfortable. Despite being an intern, I feel respected and treated as an official member of the team.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor provides very detailed guidance, explains clearly when I don't understand, and always encourages me to ask questions. The team admin supports with procedures, documents, and creates conditions for me to work smoothly.

**3. Alignment between Work and Major**  
I am studying Computer Science at school and feel it is very suitable when approaching AWS services. The work not only gives me excitement for new technical issues but also allows me to apply the knowledge I learned at school.

**4. Opportunities for Learning & Skill Development**  
During the internship, I was trained in self-learning skills, research skills, and working with AWS services. Besides, I also learned how to write reports and present work results, which are very essential skills for students.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**  
It feels great to work in an open and dynamic environment. Everyone is willing to share and help each other when facing difficulties, creating a comfortable and effective working atmosphere. This helps me integrate quickly and feel attached to the team.

**6. Policies / Benefits for Interns**  
I feel that the policies for interns are very reasonable and support learning and training. In particular, I really like the weekly Workshops organized to help program members learn from highly skilled seniors and experts in the industry.


### Other Questions
- What are you **most satisfied with** during your internship? 
    + Getting to learn knowledge from seniors and experts with many years of experience in the industry.
    + Getting to practice the necessary skills for future work.
- If recommending to friends, would you **advise them to intern here**? Why?  
    + Definitely will. FCAJ is a very good program that helps students get internship opportunities, providing a foundation and valuable connections to support them later on.


### Proposals & Wishes
